InstrumentSans-latin-wght.woff2  Instrument Sans, variable wght 400-700
IBMPlexMono-latin-400.woff2      IBM Plex Mono 400

IBMPlexMono-latin-600.woff2      IBM Plex Mono 600

Both families are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: OFL-InstrumentSans.txt
and OFL-IBMPlexMono.txt (from github.com/google/fonts, ofl/instrumentsans and
ofl/ibmplexmono). Characters outside the Latin subsets, such as Greek and
subscript digits, fall back to the system's faces, as they do on the site.
